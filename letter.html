A Little Letter for You

My love,

I can’t believe we’ve known and loved each other for two months already. Lately, we’ve been facing a lot of challenges, but I hope we continue choosing each other, no matter how cruel the world can be to us.

I’m doing my best to stay by your side, no matter what happens, because I want to be your listener whenever you have something to yap about. I want to be your travel partner, exploring new places and making beautiful memories together. I want to try new foods with you, laugh with you, and experience all the little things that make life exciting.

I want you to be my other half for eternity, and I’ll be your other half in everything. I know it might sound silly to say this now, but can we turn these dreams into reality, Ali?

I also want to apologize for the times I become grumpy or upset. Sometimes, something you do makes me feel like I’m not good enough for you. I’m sorry, Ali.

Thank you for loving me, understanding me, and staying with me despite all my flaws. Every day, you give me another reason to smile, and my love for you grows a little more.

I love you so much, my favorite raven. No matter where life takes us, a part of my heart will always belong to you.

Sincerely yours, Sky Montecalvo
